## Part I — The model in words (novel, non-Bayesian parts)

**The stage.** The world runs on a fixed finite repertoire of elementary causal mechanisms and an objective assignment saying which mechanism governs which state. The agent knows the repertoire; what he may lack is a *distinction*. His awareness gives him a coarser state space; a theory, for him, assigns one mechanism to each of his states. His model class is deliberately thin — one mechanism per category, not arbitrary mixtures — and that thinness is what makes coarseness refutable: if any distribution were expressible, no experience could ever indict his categories. Unawareness here is a missing predicate, not a missing state.

**The trigger.** Not the posterior — that is the Bayesian boundary. Under misspecification the posterior concentrates on the least-false expressible theory and confidence *grows* while everything fails; class inadequacy is not an event in the agent's algebra. So a second faculty runs beside belief: every expressible theory carries a running evidence score fed by the gap between prediction and outcome at visited situations. Under an adequate theory the score provably drifts nowhere (always-on monitoring, anytime-valid); under a theory genuinely wrong at a recurring situation it compounds. Each agent has a personal threshold — dissonance capacity, a cognitive-style dial: tolerant/slow vs trigger-happy/false-alarm-prone. Crossing it rejects that theory permanently. **Dissonance = the death of the last one**: every account the agent can state has been rejected by his own record. Fallible and indirect (he sees wreckage, never the missing predicate; in noisy worlds possibly a false alarm; in the deterministic example, exact). And play-dependent: the killing contrasts arrive only if behavior generates them — rivals differentiating inside his categories, or demand visibly punishing uniform play. Pooled play under forgiving demand conceals forever. Markets, not minds, decide whether the defect surfaces.

**The question.** The wreckage has structure: *localized* (he knows which remembered episodes refuse reconciliation), *directed* (any successful repair must reconcile the retained record), *semantically open* (nothing he can say names the answer). The question: "is there a distinction among situations I treat as alike that would reconcile my experience — and what is it?" Discipline enforced everywhere downstream: pre-insight deliberation quantifies only over currently represented objects. No prior over future theories, ever.

**Pricing the question.** He cannot value candidate answers (unrepresentable; acting belief undefined at dissonance). He *can* read his memory: he forms groupings of remembered episodes — hypotheses that certain episodes were causally alike, admissible only if some single known mechanism fits each group's data. For each coherent reading: if I could have acted separately on these groups rather than uniformly per current category, how much better would my record have gone, robustly (worst case over rival responses and over mechanisms left open)? The excess over the best single-action correction is deliberative leverage — the value of the *unresolved action margin*. Defeat per se is not what funds inquiry; an episode contributes only if he'd act differently on it once sorted. Sustained success zeroes the margin and rationally extinguishes the question. Leverage is discounted by the achievability weight — a deliberately *delabeled* probability that inquiry yields a usable distinction at all (hope without content, decaying with failure — the agent's only representation of his own unawareness) — and compared to cost. Decay delivers rational abandonment.

**Insight.** One election buys one pull of a representation-producing technology. The creative act stays a black box, but a tightly constrained one: fed the retained particulars in full concreteness (he always *had* the particulars; he lacked the predicate — insight grasps the intelligible in them); truth-blind (nothing of the objective assignment leaks in); outputs confined to minimal restorative refinements — the coarsest new distinctions under which some expressible theory fits the entire re-sorted record. Experience *directs* (must separate the irreconcilable episodes) without *determining* (several minimal repairs typically survive; record-equivalent worlds demand different ones). Hence the theorem: any economical truth-blind process must be fallible — the price of a representation that economizes on distinctions, not a defect of the inquirer.

**Refinement and return.** Success brings a new partition and, with it, recoding: he can re-sort every remembered episode under the new distinction (idealized total/infallible). Formulation (truth-blind) selects a theory consistent with the recoded record. Then the step Bayes cannot supply: old belief and new theories live on different domains; no conditioning connects them; he adopts a bridge prior — a fresh full-support prior on the expanded language. That non-uniqueness is the boundary between insight and judgment, not a defect. Ordinary judged-Bayesian life resumes; the cycle can run again.

**Warrant.** The new theory fits the record that provoked it — by construction; fitting was the entry requirement. Therefore that fit carries zero evidential weight: conditional on the full record and inquiry history, a truth-blind output is independent of which world is actual. Confidence can be total while warranted confidence is fractional — *even when the theory is true* (the row-refined firm has the same +½ gap as the column-refined one). The agent generally cannot compute his own gap: doing so requires jointly representing rival worlds his new language still cannot express. Cash value: a double-counting prohibition; validation needs fresh discriminating evidence or a broader-awareness outsider (board, investor) — the governance result.

**The close.** Acting on even a validated answer faces one more gate: the switching hurdle — learn the truth, rationally decline the switch (enlightened inertia). System-wide: refinements are finite and hope decays, so inquiry dies out almost surely — answered, unpriced, abandoned, or unanswerable — and where each agent's questioning died fixes the language, theory, and payoff he keeps. Heterogeneity originates in question histories, before any prior exists to disagree about.

## Part II — Conceptual clarifications from the Aug 16 discussion

**1. Agent's states are primitives; partitions are analyst bookkeeping.**
Author's position: Nature holds the most refined space (states = full snapshots implying causal consequences); the firm's space is lower-ranked; God's-eye it is a partition of Nature's space, but *to the firm its states are the primitives* — it cannot think below them (contrast with Schipper-style lattices where the mapping apparatus is in view).
Resolution: the formalism already enforces this. The measurability discipline (Table 1, observation assumption) means nothing the agent computes with ever mentions Nature's states: he perceives cells, his objects are defined on cells, his record stores episodes without state labels, he knows cell probabilities with no access to any decomposition. He lives on the quotient space; the partition is the analyst's device for relating his space to Nature's. The lattice machinery is dispensable here because (i) no interactive unawareness and (ii) common mechanism vocabulary.
Caveat found: the manuscript's sentence "An awareness cell is thus an implicit causal claim" (awareness subsection) speaks God's-eye; defensible as analyst commentary, now flagged for Session 4 (see Part III).

**2. The token/type device — the model's central trick for respecting point 1.**
At dissonance the agent entertains "things I filed together may differ" — but over *episodes*, not states. He cannot partition his cells (nothing exists below them, from his side); he CAN partition his memory. Deliberative groupings sort remembered particulars, individuated by when they happened, not by what they were. Insight = converting a token-grouping into a predicate — abstraction from particulars, formalized. For §2: the firm's reflective access runs over remembered wins and losses, never over finer types.

**3. "The language" — defined.**
= the agent's state space (cells) PLUS the theory class expressible on it (one mechanism per cell). A represented mathematical object. "The language fails" = every member of that class rejected by the agent's own record (active set empty). The inferential step beyond the event: "my space is missing a distinction" is a THEOREM in the deterministic exact case, an operational fallible diagnosis in the stochastic case. Term is replaceable ("representation," "causal frame," "world view") so long as glossed once; author's discretion when editing the abstract.

**4. The trigger, stochastic reading — author's version amended.**
Author: agent tracks failure episodes until the failure rate is statistically unlikely given his tolerance, then questions. Correct with three amendments: (a) per-theory scores; questioning begins at CLASS EXHAUSTION — the last theory's death, not the first; single-theory rejection is ordinary model rejection within his world; (b) monitoring is sequential/anytime-valid — always-on peeking without false-alarm inflation; (c) two tolerance dials — a misfit deadband and the accumulated-evidence capacity threshold; rejection permanent within the current language.

**5. Color/speed example = the model, one rung up.**
A firm aware of speed but not color IS the column-refined firm in a row-law world (validation-stretch loser; both trap firms). Correction: the firm sees *episode*-specific consequences, not state-specific — which color each sale involved was never recorded (no predicate to record with); the analyst record keeps the labels. Recodability = once "color" is possessed, remembered sales can be re-sorted by it. "Investigating history asking what am I missing" = forming and pricing coherent groupings of remembered episodes; only the technology, not the investigation, can deliver "color."

**6. The Thomistic/Lonergan resonance — where the model quietly agrees.**
(a) The technology consumes retained particulars in full concreteness — insight as an operation on experience, not conjuring: the intelligible grasped in the phantasm. (b) Questions are anomaly-generated, hence pre-anchored in a real defect — and in the deterministic case it is a THEOREM that every dissonant record is repairable: every question the model can pose provably has an answer; under noise typically but not always. Formal echo of "we tend to ask questions that have answers." (c) The achievability weight is exactly the loose, contentless sense that there is something out there to learn — disciplined by decay into rational abandonment when misplaced. No explicit Thomistic appeal needed in the paper; the cognitional subsection carries the mapping, the math carries the substance.

**7. "What the answer is worth" — TWO claims, keep them separate (biggest §2 risk).**
- Act 2 claim (fallibility/direction-without-determination): the delivered distinction may be the wrong one; and even a genuinely restorative repair can be objectively insufficient (the isolating repairs reconcile the record while still pooling causally different states — later play can re-expose; the cycle runs again). Awareness cumulative; progress real; completeness never certified. This is the author's "other dimensions may still be at play" reading — true, and it belongs to Act 2.
- Act 4 claim (warrant): sharper and stranger — EVEN IF the distinction is exactly right, the confidence is unearned. Retrospective fit adds zero evidential weight because organizing the record was the entry requirement for being produced. The row-refined (true) firm carries the same +½ gap. "Worth" = evidential worth. Protect the sentence: **certainty without validation — and the firm cannot audit itself.**

## Part III — Writing guidance distilled (abstract + §2)

- Narrate §2 from INSIDE the firm: episodes remembered, wins and losses, categories as the only reality it has. Analyst state labels appear only when stepping outside to say what was really going on (trap, knife edge).
- Prefer "every account the firm can state has failed its own record" over "the language failed," or gloss "language" once (see II.3).
- Trigger phrasing: last theory dies, under an always-on tally with a personal tolerance.
- Act 2 vs Act 4: direction-without-determination there; generation-is-not-validation here. Do not blend.
- Token/type discipline: the firm sorts memories, not states; insight turns a sorting into a predicate.
